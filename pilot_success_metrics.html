SAP Runs on SAP — EC Benefits Pilot: Success Metrics

Pilot: Jan 13 – Apr 30, 2027 GA Target: August 2027 For review: Stephanie · Jill · Kirstae Draft — Oct 21, 2026
North Star
Build confidence in the August 2027 EC Benefits rollout by engaging pilot participants within SuccessFactors to test out real-world benefit scenarios in EC Benefits, and pressure test that EC Benefits is prepared to support 17,000 US SAP employees at GA.

Feedback Classification — All feedback routes into one of three buckets
Bucket 1
Base Function
Does it work as designed?
"I tried X, expected Y, got Z."
Bucket 2
Enhancement Ideas
Works, but the experience could be better.
"This works, but it would be easier if…"
Bucket 3
Net New Capabilities
Capabilities not yet on the roadmap.
"I wish I could…" or "It would be great if…"

  • Feedback itemsVariable / unpredictable
  • Scenario coverageNot applicable
  • Time to first feedbackSame week (quality uncertain)

Option 2
Coffee Drip
  • Active participants5–15 / month (organic)
  • Feedback items20–60 total

Visibility Path

Hybrid

Open Questions

  • What is engineering's H1 2027 capacity for pilot feedback — needed to set the H1 Incorporation Rate target
  • What survey tool captures feedback at end of the EC Benefits session? Survey questions must map to the 3-bucket framework before Wave 1
  • Who owns AI-assisted feedback triage, and what is the SLA for routing to Jira or Aha?
  • Would this test population need to continue testing post-H1 2027 — all participants, a subset, or an additional ~6-week window before GA?
